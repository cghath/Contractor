## R05 DcNZmXauw00 - thee.foxhole - "How you walk into an ambush... Professionally" (React to Ambush, near vs far) - 84 s
Caption (brief): "Drop, take cover, return fire?" is a fatal mistake in a NEAR ambush. NEAR ambush = within 35 m (hand-grenade range): you are inside a pre-sighted kill zone with no time for cover or breaking contact; kill-zone soldiers immediately return violent suppressive fire, throw grenades and assault straight through the enemy line before the ambush consolidates. FAR ambush (> 35 m): distance gives reaction time; the element in the kill zone drops, takes cover, returns heavy suppressive fire and establishes a BASE OF FIRE while the uncommitted element maneuvers to flank and destroy the ambush line. History: British SAS / Commonwealth forces, Malayan Emergency 1948-60 (cover in close ambush = near 100% casualties), first "Immediate Action" drills; spread via Malayan Jungle Warfare School, US 5th SFG Recondo School, LRRPs; codified as US Army Battle Drill 4: React to Ambush.

Echelon: fire team / squad (a "team in the kill zone" splits into support and assault).

# SYNTHESIS (from R01-R10, all thee.foxhole; no 0.25 s passes were needed: the animations are narrated and every state change was readable at 1 s)
Evidence strength: R01-R06 give squad/fire-team drills (strong, repeated); R07-R08 give company-and-above ideas (single source each, plus the caption); R09-R10 give structure only. Numbers marked (est.) are my translation of cartoon distances ("body-lengths") into game metres: 1 body-length is about 1.5-2 m, and the cartoon scale is not exact, so tune in playtest.

## A. Constants that apply at every echelon
1. Never bunch. Walking in a column or huddle gets a whole team killed by one burst or grenade (R01 0-25 s, R03 8-25 s, R04 7-21 s, R05 7-15 s). AI rule: while moving in the open, every man keeps >= 4-5 body-lengths (est. 6-10 m) from the nearest friend; the spacing check must also hold when taking cover (no two men on the same piece of cover unless it is a bound pair).
2. Dispersion exists so that each man reaches cover immediately on contact (R01 49 s).
3. Every action ends in a defined end state ("done"): consolidated, all-round security, a rally point, or a limit of advance (R01, R03, R04).
4. Use smoke to remove the enemy's line of sight before a move or a breach (R04, R05, R06).
5. Do not treat a contact the same everywhere: range (35 m), cover, enemy fortification and troops available change the drill (R05, R06, R03 "METT-TC").
6. Fire superiority first, movement second; movement without covering fire is "suicide", fire without movement "wastes ammo" (R03 64-72 s).

## B. FIRE TEAM (4-6 men)
Roster (R04 24 s, R09 46-51 s): team leader (TL), automatic rifleman (AR/SAW), grenadier (GRN, 40 mm), rifleman (RFLM); US Army team = 4 (R10 chart: TL M4, GRN M203, AR M249, RFLM M4). 5-6 = add a designated marksman or a medic/extra rifleman.

B1. Patrol movement (R01 35-48 s, R04 24-47 s)
- Trigger: team moving, no known contact.
- Formation: staggered wedge/diamond; rifleman as point with eyes up; TL behind and central; AR and GRN on the flanks; a rear/mid man navigates (R04 51-56 s: the point man never looks at the map).
- Behaviour: cover-to-cover bounds at a reasonable pace, ~5 body-lengths apart, with a pre-designated rally point (RP) named before leaving ("if things go bad, run to that rock feature, point Alpha", R04 32-37 s). Done = reached next cover with all four observing their arcs.
- Implementation: the squad AI should pick an RP (a rock, wall, building corner behind the team) every ~100-200 m (est.) and tell the team; the RP must have cover and an outward-facing perimeter spots.

B2. Contact report, three D's (R04 64-71 s)
- Trigger: first man to see/take fire.
- Says: "Contact" then direction (clock position), description, distance e.g. "12 o'clock, 75 metres, 3 hostiles".
- Gain: the point man who is scanning gets about half a second more to dive into cover (R04 58-62 s).
- Done = every teammate knows bearing, range and count; TL then calls the drill.

B3. Immediate reaction to contact (R01 49-56 s)
- Each man goes to the nearest cover (a different one each), stops, returns accurate fire; base-of-fire volume maintained. Do NOT stop to treat in the open (R04 bad case: three men panic or buddy-aid, team is enveloped).
- Near ambush (within ~35 m, hand-grenade range, R05 24-57 s): no cover-seeking. Everyone returns violent suppressive fire at once, throws grenades, and the free men assault straight through the enemy line (grenade first, then charge in 2-3 s). Trigger conditions: first shot from < 35 m and the team is inside a pre-registered kill zone (enemy line visible, on the near side).
- Far ambush (> 35 m): drop, take cover, heavy suppressive fire, pop smoke between, form a base of fire; uncommitted men flank (R05 58-79 s).

B4. Break contact (Battle Drill 3, R04 64-103 s)
- Trigger: TL decides the fight is untenable (casualties, outnumbered, no cover); TL calls "Break contact".
- Sequence (verbatim from the reel): (1) #2 (grenadier) throws smoke between the team and the enemy (cloud ~15 m wide). (2) #3 and #4 (AR + one rifleman) keep firing, with automatic fire plus accurate single shots to keep heads down. (3) when the fight is controlled, the man furthest back (#1) falls back first: he yells "Moving", bounds, hits cover, yells "Set". (4) on "Set", #2 says "Moving" and bounds; then #3 and #4 each say "Moving" and go (also covered by whoever has "Set"). (5) all arrive at RP Alpha: defensive perimeter, then hold or push back.
- Done = all men "Set" at RP, perimeter facing the enemy, ammo/casualty check; an AI that cannot see the enemy may lift the smoke and wait.
- Timing: no values given; use 3-5 s per bound (est., from "I'm up, he sees me, I'm down").

B5. Bounding (R03 57-77 s)
- Buddy pair moves under the "I'm up, he sees me, I'm down" cadence: man A in cover firing accurately, man B sprints to the next cover, drops, fires; then swap. One of the pair is always firing. Exposed time kept to ~3-5 s.

B6. Support by fire (as a fire team or half squad, R02 8-100 s)
- Position: choose cover with concealment and a field of fire onto the enemy; spacing ~3-5 body-lengths in a line abreast; low posture (prone/kneel) behind cover.
- Sectors: each man gets a wedge ~30-40 deg; adjacent wedges overlap; together they cover the whole enemy position AND extend to both its flanks (so the enemy cannot reinforce or withdraw); never all on the same target (R02 18-37 s: "10 rifles on one guy" is wrong).
- Weapons: AR + GL deliver sustained controlled automatic fire and grenades (R02 78-83 s).
- Pattern avoidance: after a few shots, change the firing position (move left/right/lower, reload and clear malfunctions in cover) because a marksman or HMG waiting will kill a man who reappears from the same spot (R02 56-66 s).
- Shift and lift (R03 82-105 s): as the assault element approaches, SBF shifts the point of concentration to the area beside/ahead of the objective on the assault's side ("shift"), then ceases all fire ("lift") when the assault is on the objective. The assault is marked by a flag or IR marker on the point man (R06 144-151 s) or the SL calls it. Done = SBF silent, all eyes on flanks and rear.

B7. Assault in lanes (R03 106-146 s)
- Each assaulter gets a lane ~3-4 body-lengths wide (est. 6 m), do not huddle, do not cheat the lane to go around an obstacle (leaves bypassed enemies, throws out of line, crowds teammates), clear your own sector.
- Stop at the limit of advance (LOA): a line drawn beyond the objective; never move away from your support or outside the unit's area of operations.
- Done = objective swept, all men consolidated and facing out ("secure and disciplined").

B8. Trench/bunker CQB for a team (R06 152-188 s)
- Trench: roll the line from the flank; lead man hugs the inside wall and slices corners; second man covers overheads/long stretches; never cross an open bend without pie-ing the angle. Bunker entry: expect a 90-degree baffle wall behind the door; first throw smoke through the entrance, then throw a grenade from a strong wall position, then clear; mark each cleared room with a VS-17 panel or coloured smoke (R06 176-188 s).
- Done = marker placed on the cleared room.

## C. SQUAD (8-12 men: SL + 2-3 teams)
Rosters seen: US Army 9 = SL + two teams of 4 (R10 chart); USMC 15 = SL, ASL, systems operator + three teams of 4 (R10 table). For the game: squad of 8-12 = SL (+ optional ASL) + 2 teams.
Movement elements per R10 (caption + narration): Assault (main effort, closes, breaches, kills), Support (support by fire, suppress), Security (guard, screen, protect, block flanks and rear, prevent reinforcement). A 9-man squad has only two teams: security must be a detail from the support team or the leader's own element.

C1. Formation on the move (R01 35-44 s)
- Lead fire team in a wedge up front (concerned only with moving from cover to cover at a reasonable pace), SL in the middle, second team (assault element) trailing ~3-4 body-lengths (est. 5-8 m) behind in a loose line.

C2. React to Contact, Battle Drill 1 (R01 49-80 s, FM Fig. 4-4 at 29-34 s)
- Trigger: lead team receives fire. Immediate: lead team goes to cover, returns fire (base of fire). No talking required (a drill, practised until automatic).
- SL moves forward to the lead team, assesses, and chooses: (a) flank with the assault element (usually around one side in cover), (b) assault element bounds forward under the base of fire, or (c) support-and-assault. The assault element goes around the flank away from the line of fire (R01 69-72 s: curved route to the right) and sets up to provide covering fire or assault; grenades/fires on the enemy at 75 s; enemy neutralised one at a time (76-77 s).
- Done = enemy position cleared or suppressed with the squad consolidated; if enemy too strong -> B4.

C3. Deliberate squad assault (R03 26-146 s)
- Preconditions: Do not move until the support-by-fire position is established and firing and the SL gives the signal (R03 34-39 s).
- Choice of attack: frontal is fine once the enemy is overloaded by suppression; flank costs time and exposes to friendly fire; choose by METT-TC (R03 40-54 s).
- Flow: set conditions -> SL signal -> assault bounds in lanes (buddy teams, cover to cover) -> SBF shifts then lifts (SL calls "shift", "lift" or the marker is seen) -> assault clears in lanes -> LOA stop -> consolidate and all-round security. SBF "must be hyper-aware of the assault element's position to prevent fratricide".

C4. Near/far ambush at squad level (R05)
- Near: the team inside the kill zone splits at once, without orders, into support (cyclic fire) and assault (frag + concussion grenades onto the enemy line, then a straight charge through the ambush); it immediately consolidates security on the far side. Far: kill-zone men drop, base of fire + smoke; uncommitted men (the second team) flank the ambush from the side (R05 58-79 s).

C5. Bunker or trench (R06; the caption calls it a platoon problem)
- Standalone bunker, flank possible (R06 40-81 s): split the squad; fire element in hard cover suppresses firing ports with heavy automatic fire to make the gunner "come off the sight"; smoke directly in front of the firing slits; flank element approaches through dead space (side with no slits) using cover; grenades through the front slit and back door, then immediate breach and clear survivors.
- Complex (trench, wire, mines; no flank): SOSRA (R06 92-141 s). Suppress (all fires on the bunker and neighbouring trench positions, focus on the MG and the designated marksman on the gunner), Obscure (smoke along the trench slits), Secure (support element moves forward to secure the breach and kill near threats on the flanks), Reduce (breach team: Bangalore torpedoes and satchels on the wire/mines), Assault (through the breach, into the trench; a designated team holds security at communication-trench junctions and the breach).

C6. Squad on a base-of-fire ratio: roughly one-half to two-thirds of the squad's firepower (AR, GL, DM) goes to the SBF; assault takes the riflemen and grenadier; SL placed where he can see both (R01 mid-column; R03 SL communicates with SBF).

## D. PLATOON (24-48 men: PL, PSG, 3 squads, optionally weapons squad)
- Only two direct references: the FM Fig. 4-4 platoon panel in R01 (29-34 s; a line of squads with a flanking arc and HQ at the rear; labels illegible) and R06's SOSRA caption. R10: "fire teams are squad and platoon level tactics".
- Implied TTP (R06 SOSRA scaled up): one squad = support/base of fire (with weapons squad and DM), one squad = breach/assault, one squad = secure (isolates the objective, cuts lateral movement, covers breach and junctions); PL controls the lift/shift.
- Marking: IR/flag marker on the lead man of the assaulting unit (R06) so the SBF knows when to shift.

## E. COMPANY (~160-240 men) AND BRIGADE (sum of companies + command element)
E1. Dispersed attack against a fortified village (R07 8-63 s, TC 7-100.2)
- Trigger: defender is entrenched with drones/ISR and mass movement is lethal.
- Phase 1 Fix and attrit: indirect fires, glide bombs and loitering munitions pin the garrison (R07 33-39 s). Phase 2 Isolate: small dispersed teams (2-3 dismounts or ATGM/drone teams) infiltrate along tree lines, gullies, micro-terrain to the flanks, set a security zone and interdict supply routes (MSR/GLOC), rotation routes (R07 40-51 s). Phase 3 Starve: the garrison runs out of ammo, men and equipment (52-57 s). Phase 4 Concentrate at the decisive point only at the end: converge rapidly from several dispersed axes (58-63 s).
- AI: the company AI needs an "isolate before assault" state machine with explicit supply-route targets; each detachment picks the least-observed approach (concealed axes) not the shortest; a final convergence timer starts only when the garrison reports low ammo / no reinforcements.

E2. Defence in depth with a security zone (R08 20-84 s)
- Soviet-style: security zone (recon + ATGM + special forces, from the second echelon) forward of the battle zone and support zone (artillery) behind a defensive line; purpose: force the attacker to deploy or attack prematurely in a disadvantageous direction and feed artillery; artillery is the main killer; Soviet divisions fight with a 1:1 artillery:manoeuvre ratio vs US 3 BCT : 1 DIVARTY. Drone/EW "eyes" monitor terrain in real time.
- AI: a defending company places a forward screen (2-3 small recon/ATGM elements, 2-5 km ahead (est.)), a battle zone of 3 mutually supporting positions and an artillery/fires cell behind; screen elements call fires and displace instead of fighting to the death.

E3. Feigned retreat and fix-flank at company/battalion scale (R04 105-121 s)
- Use BD3 as a weapon: pull back or give ground to drag defenders into preset kill zones at RPs, or pull defenders off the objective while a second force pushes in (map: north force retreats, defenders chase, west/south forces enter the town and kill).
- AI: if the objective's garrison reacts to a withdrawing unit by leaving its position, the commander triggers the "push in" element.

## F. TOP-LEVEL AI IMPLICATIONS (priority order)
1. Dispersion + immediate cover on contact (all echelons).
2. Contact report in three D's, then a leader-called drill.
3. Near ambush (<= 35 m): charge through; far ambush: base of fire + flank (+ smoke).
4. Base of fire first; manoeuvre element moves only after SBF is firing and the leader signals.
5. Bounding (up/see/down), one man always firing; "Moving"/"Set" calls in break contact.
6. Sectors of fire that overlap and extend past both flanks; no focus-fire on one man; relocate firing position; shift then lift on the assault's arrival.
7. Lanes and LOA for assaulters; consolidate with all-round security.
8. Smoke before moving or breaching; mark cleared rooms.
9. Pre-designated rally points and fallback points before moving.
10. Higher echelon: fix with fires, isolate (MSR), then concentrate; feigned retreat; security zone + artillery.
