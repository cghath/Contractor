## What's built

| Area | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Seasons | `scripts/survival/seasons.gd` (`Seasons`), `Survival.season()` | 4 seasons x 10 world days from the New world option "season" (spring default; NewWorldScreen's Season row). Weather rolled per season every 6 world hours into DayNight (winter "snow" = overcast + `Seasons.snow()`). Body-lane flags: `thirst_mult()` (summer 1.5), `spoil_mult()` (how long food keeps: summer 0.5, winter 2), `water_frozen()`, `temperature_c()`, `heat()`. The dead's pace `dead_pace_mult()` (winter 0.75, hard frost 0.5, summer 1.1) in ZombieAI and ZoneDead. Hard frost (winter nights, winter days 3-8): the crowd's heads frozen (stand still), live dead outdoors frozen (no move, no bite, one hit shatters: their pool is set to nothing). The spring thaw: every frozen head wakes as a horde at the settlement (or the noise), and every far zone that froze gets a "thaw" event and pressure 85. Summer wildfires (15% a day: an event and pressure elsewhere). Farming: planting window (spring + summer's first 3 days) takes a seed packet per 2 beds, the harvest window (rest of summer, autumn) yields, winter none; a missed window means no harvest that year. The world sim's season follows the option (`w.season_start`); `Seasons.bias_options` on every group's goals: autumn settlers forage/work +0.25, autumn raider raids +0.35, winter raids -0.1, spring scavengers to cities/towns +0.3, enclave patrols summer +0.2 / winter -0.2, a lit settlement raises raiders' gain on the players (footprint / 120). `WorldTrade.season` set. |
| Raids and sieges | `scripts/survival/sieges.gd` (`Sieges`, a node on the zone) | At a wall (ZombieAI calls `server_at_wall` when stuck): crawlers squeeze through a gap under a wall, runners vault up to 1 m, the rest pile (0.3 m a head per wall column) and climb when the pile reaches the wall's height, chew wood (`Builder.server_chew_at`; scrap/brick/concrete/steel hold), push gates and doors (0.5 hp/s a head). Horde siege: at night, pressure >= 80 within 200 m of the settlement: 3 waves of 15-30 every 120 s walking at the settlement (ZoneDead heads with "to"), dawn ends it with a journal toll and "siege_survived". Live raid ("assault" groups from `WorldGoals._raid_player`, WorldLive's bodies): support fire on a lookout (2 hp/s within 80 m), a breach charge on the nearest wood wall (ZoneLevel.server_breach) 8 s in within 30 m, lost after 45 s inside the radius with no player there (40% of every store, a settler hurt, one captive with 3+ settlers, wood walls hurt), repelled when all down ("raid_repelled"). |
| Politics | `scripts/survival/politics.gd` (`Politics`), Council.factions | Factions: family, the pious (new), old crew, newcomers; shared "faction_low"/"faction_high" by the faction's mean mood (<35 / >65). Theft at 02:00: hunger < 35, mood < 40, 30%: 2 food taken, "stole" / "theft". Split at 12:00: a faction (not the whole settlement) with mean mood < 25 and a leader-trait member leaves as a world group (`WorldModel.group_from_people`) with a grudge. The challenge's contest `server_contest(how)`: standing (player weight vs the challenger's backers), duel (melee level x10 + roll vs courage and traits + roll), vote (settlers by opinion vs relation), leave; lost: the settlement passes (`settlement_lost` kept, the settlers a world group with the settlement as home). Pacts in the world section: alliance (+40, warnings of hordes/raids/demands near the settlement, `server_call_allies`), protection (+20, 10% of their food a day), feud (<= -60, a grudge). |
| Infrastructure | `scripts/survival/infrastructure.gd` (`Infrastructure`) | Sources: generator 3 (fuel in stock), solar panel 1 x sun (0 at night, less under cloud), wind turbine 1.5 (0.3 in calm), battery banks 4 each (charged hourly from spare). Wiring is reach: within 3.5 m of anything on the network. Consumers by priority: electric fence 1, fridge 0.6, water pump 0.8, lamp 0.25, power tools 1. Lamps light only with power (the footprint's light; a lit settlement raises the zone's pressure at night). `fridge_on()`, `pump_on()` (the body lane), `build_mult()` (1.5 with powered tools), `powered_here()` (FallenRadio's power check). Projects worked by the maintain job: water works (24 h; calls the body lane's `WaterWorks.server_repaired(zone)` when that class exists), dam (96 h: zone powered, a world event), clear a road (8 h), repair a bridge (16 h). Bridges: `server_blow_bridge(a, b)` closes the crossing (`WorldModel.crossing_open`): goals, journeys and far hordes go round. |
| Traps | `scripts/survival/traps.gd` (`Traps`) | Spike pit kills (15 hp wear a kill), wire holds walkers 8 s and is chewed (1.5 hp/s a head), bear trap holds 20 s then needs "Reset the trap", a powered electric fence stuns 4 s; drawbridge (a flat door), kill corridor markers (defence 2). `Traps.holds()` stops ZombieAI. |
| Lures | `scripts/survival/lures.gd` (`Lures`) | Thrown `glass_bottle` (Throwables "bottle": 35 m), `server_car_alarm` (60 s, 120 m), the lure_radio piece "Leave it playing" (300 s, 60 m every 4 s), burning flares at night (80 m), the goat_stake piece (bleats every 6 s, bitten to death). |
| Masking and bodies | `scripts/survival/masking.gd` (`Masking`), TalkMenu | "Smear its gore on you" on a dead one (2 smears a corpse): `gore_until` meta (600 s), no smell, 0.3 of their sight at night while slow; `gore_at` for the body lane's hygiene and infection. Piles of 4+ corpses within 6 m past 24 world hours raise pressure and may breed a bloater; the pyre piece burns corpses within 6 m (noise and pressure: draws others); the haul job buries 3 an hour. |

## What's left

- The car alarm has no wreck interaction yet (`Lures.server_car_alarm` is the API for the vehicles lane's menu).
- Bridges are blown and repaired through the API (`Infrastructure.server_blow_bridge`, the bridge project);
  a demolitions charge on a real bridge in a zone isn't bound to it; roads cleared are a flag only.
- The zone def doesn't carry the season to the terrain yet (ZoneFeatures' snow and crops by season):
  one line in `Survival._host_zone` (`def["season"] = season()`), held back to keep the other suites' terrain.
- Raiders' orders stay WorldLive's (attack the nearest player); support by fire and the breach are
  rules on top, not SquadAI orders of their own; a vehicle through a gate is later.
- The dam has no place in the zone (a project record with a flag); the enclave wanting it is a world event only.
- Weather fronts and the barometer (call 91), seasonal fauna (103), wild edibles (105), spreading fire (107).
- The body lane reads: `Survival.season()`, `Seasons.thirst_mult/spoil_mult/water_frozen/temperature_c`,
  `Infrastructure.fridge_on/pump_on/water_works_on`, the `gore_until`/`gore_at` metas.
