# Contractor: Fallen: Vision

Status: draft for Captain, 2026-11-20. Sources: cheeno_handoff.md, survival.md, survival_firsthour.md, the 2026-11-20 gate reports.

## Pitch

Survive an overrun open world on a farm of your own, where the dead and the living keep moving, fleeing, trading and starving whether or not you are watching.

## Who it's for

A solo player who loves long-run survival and settlement games and wants a world with its own life. They accept slow, consequence-heavy play, read the journal, and like a companion at their side. Two to four player co-op is supported, not the design focus.

## Pillars

1. **A world that lives without you.** Households hold or flee the dead, convoys drive and get robbed, refugees ask to stay, far places keep simulating. *A feature serves this pillar if it makes the world do something when the player is absent.*
2. **Hands-on survival, no hand-holding.** Needs, fieldcraft, building, gunsmithing, tanning, wear and jams, taught by the world and a once-only hint, never a tutorial. *A feature serves this pillar if the player learns it by doing it and pays a real cost for neglect.*
3. **People, not statistics.** Companions, children, bonds, horses, a settlement with a vote. *A feature serves this pillar if losing it would hurt a named person or animal.*
4. **Quiet is dangerous.** Sparse open-country dead, noise that draws only the near ones, nights that ramp, seasons that turn. *A feature serves this pillar if it makes the player's choices (noise, light, time) matter more than a spawn timer.*

## Core loop

- **Minute to minute:** scavenge and move quietly, fight only when you must, with melee and noise as real tradeoffs.
- **Session:** farm, scavenge, fortify, survive the night. The first day has five goals: water, a better weapon, shelter, the old pickup running, and day 2's radio survivor.
- **Campaign:** grow the household and settlement through seasons, trading and standing with other camps, while the world sim moves on.

## What makes it ours

Honest comparables: Project Zomboid (long-run survival), State of Decay 2 (settlement and people), DayZ and SCUM (scavenging, wear), RimWorld (bonds), Kenshi (world sim). We differ in combining a world-sim of fleeing and holding households, bonded pairs, live convoys and worn, jamming guns with the Contractor wound model (blood, vessels, no HP) in a voxel world. The first-hour onboarding follows the shape of State of Decay and Zomboid; we make it ours with a real farmstead, goals that pay small XP, and hints in the player's own keys. The mod kit and world cards are data-only.

## Tone and feel

Grim and grounded. Quiet, scarce and heavy. Information lives in the world, the body screen and the journal, not a HUD. Accessibility (colour-blind-safe tells, text size, subtitles, solo pause) is part of the feel.

## What v1 means

v1 is a free, invite-only closed playtest, no store. **Everything stays in v1**: children, horses and farriers, gunsmithing, tanning, bonds, seasons, settlement and the full world sim. The goal is to make it cohere. The bar for each system: finished, tuned (no "Proposed" number left in the core loop), tested (survival and firsthour suites green repeatedly) and playtested. Honest status: NO-GO at the 2026-11-20 gate; the only recorded playtest said "not fun yet", and the melee swing has no tell.

## Non-goals

- No chosen-one story and no scripted tutorial.
- No endless horde spawns; the wild stays sparse.
- No store, no public release in v1.
- No unsaved progress: quitting must save.
- No mod scripting; mods are data-only.

## Captain's decisions (2026-11-20)

1. **No cuts:** everything built stays in v1.
2. **Solo first,** co-op a bonus.
3. **v1 is a free closed playtest** (invite-only, no store).
4. **All at once:** every system (children, horses, gunsmithing, seasons, settlement, the world sim, not just the first hour) reaches the bar before the first outside session.
5. **Playtesters are Cheeno and friends:** people who didn't build what they're testing. Informal, but every session leaves notes (and the session log) that go through `/feedback`.

## Captain's decisions (2026-11-21)

6. **The playtest invite is solo only.** Co-op stays in the build but is not offered to testers, since no Fallen co-op run exists yet.
7. **"Fun" means more interactive and more intense.** A fight is fun when the player keeps making choices during it (not only before it) and the pressure rises while they do. This is the test for melee, gun feel and every M1 change: does it give the player more to do in the fight, or make the fight harder to get out of?
